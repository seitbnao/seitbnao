## About me

I am a **Software Engineer** and researcher at the **University of Campinas (UNICAMP)**, working at the intersection of **computing, engineering and interdisciplinary research**.

My work focuses on the development of computational methods, scientific software and decision-support tools for real-world problems involving environmental systems, territorial analysis, logistics, human factors and health-related applications.

I am particularly interested in transforming research methods into **reproducible, reusable and accessible computational tools**.

## Current research

### Socio-environmental risk and territorial systems

My current research investigates computational approaches for evaluating **socio-environmental risk, logistics, territorial exposure, vulnerability and response capacity** in port and waterway systems.

One of the main application areas is the **Marajó Bay estuarine region in the Brazilian Amazon**, integrating environmental, socioeconomic, logistical and geospatial information.

`Geospatial Data` · `Environmental Indicators` · `Port Logistics` · `Territorial Analysis` · `MCDA`

---

### Human–Exoskeleton Interaction

I also work with biomechanical indicators and multicriteria decision-support methods applied to the evaluation of passive upper-limb exoskeletons.

**IMEB-Exo Synthetic Dataset**

`Biomechanical Signals` · `Occupational Ergonomics` · `CRITIC` · `TOPSIS` · `FlowSort` · `Decision Support`
